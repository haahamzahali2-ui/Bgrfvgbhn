Connect a Google Sheet

One-time setup, about 5 minutes. Your data goes to a Sheet only you own.
  1. Make a new Google Sheet. Go to sheets.new and name it something like MCAT Prep Tracker.
  2. Open the script editor. In the Sheet's menu: Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Paste the script. Delete everything in the editor, then paste this and click 💾 Save:
  4. Deploy it. Click Deploy → New deployment, click the ⚙️ next to "Select type" and pick Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  5. Allow access. Google asks you to authorize. Pick your account → Advanced → Go to (project name) → Allow. (It says "unverified" because you wrote it — it's your own script.)
  6. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it into ⚙️ Settings, and hit Connect.
📱 Other devices: paste the same URL into Settings there — your data loads automatically.
🔒 Keep the URL private: anyone who has it can see and change your data.
↩️ Undo-safe: Google Sheets keeps version history (File → Version history).
